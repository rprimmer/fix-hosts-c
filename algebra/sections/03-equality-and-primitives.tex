\section{Equality and Primitive Transformations}

\subsection{Equality Relations}

\begin{definition}[Full equality]
$S=T$ requires equality of every modeled field, including backup history and
allow-list ordering.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Hosts-and-allow-list equivalence]
\[
  S \HAequiv T
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  S.H=T.H \land S.A=T.A.
\]
This relation ignores backups, metadata, cache state, and traces.
\end{definition}

Let $\SetOf{A}$ discard ordering and duplicate lines from an allow list.

\begin{definition}[Setwise equivalence]
\[
  S \Setequiv T
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  S.H=T.H \land \SetOf{S.A}=\SetOf{T.A}.
\]
\end{definition}

These relations prevent the word \emph{idempotent} from concealing which parts
of an execution are actually being observed.

\subsection{Tokens Before a Comment}

$\Tokens(l)$ splits a line on whitespace and stops at the first hash character.
For example,

\begin{align*}
\Tokens(&\text{``0.0.0.0 ads.example.com tracker.example.com''})\\
  &=\langle\text{``0.0.0.0''},\text{``ads.example.com''},
             \text{``tracker.example.com''}\rangle,\\[0.5em]
\Tokens(&\text{``0.0.0.0 ads.example.com \# local''})\\
  &=\langle\text{``0.0.0.0''},\text{``ads.example.com''}\rangle,\\[0.5em]
\Tokens(&\text{``\# ads.example.com''})=\langle\rangle.
\end{align*}

Exact active membership is defined by

\[
  \Contains(d,l) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad d\in\Tokens(l).
\]

Thus \code{ads.example.com} is not contained in a line whose only relevant
token is \code{notads.example.com}.

\subsection{Hosts-File Removal}

For $d\in\DNSName$, $\Remove{d}:\Hosts\rightarrow\Hosts$ removes every line
containing $d$ as an exact active token:

\begin{align*}
\Remove{d}(\langle\rangle) &= \langle\rangle,\\
\Remove{d}(l::hs) &=
  \begin{cases}
    \Remove{d}(hs), & \Contains(d,l),\\
    l::\Remove{d}(hs), & \neg\Contains(d,l).
  \end{cases}
\end{align*}

Retained lines preserve both relative order and byte contents.

\subsection{Allow-List Insertion}

For $d\in\DNSName$, $\Insert{d}:\AllowList\rightarrow\AllowList$ appends $d$
only when an exactly equal line is absent:

\[
\Insert{d}(A)=
  \begin{cases}
    A, & d\in\Lines(A),\\
    A\concat\langle d\rangle, & d\notin\Lines(A).
  \end{cases}
\]

The complete-line interpretation corresponds to the implementation's use of
\code{fileContainsLine()} rather than substring search.
